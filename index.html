🗺 Leaflet Route Builder

Turn a ward's road data into printable, walkable leaflet-delivery routes -- no installation needed, works from any Google Sheet.

1Load your road data

The address of the ward's live leaflet-map site. Reads its data source, backend, and title automatically -- nothing else on this page needs filling in by hand.
⚙ Load from a plain Google Sheet instead
In your Sheet: File → Share → Publish to web → select the right tab → format CSV → Publish. Paste the link it gives you.

2Choose ward and route size

~90 minutes of leafleting at ~100 homes/hour. Smaller = easier, more routes.

4Connect this ward's live tracker (optional)

From the live leaflet-map site's own config (MAP_CONFIG.APPS_SCRIPT_URL in its index.html). In-app progress reports write straight to that ward's own Data sheet -- authorised editors write directly, everyone else's taps go to that site's normal pending-review queue. Leave blank to generate a read-only app with no reporting.

5Get your deliverables

A fresh route plan should be generated close to the actual event day so it reflects the latest leafleted roads -- publishing here auto-deletes any previously published page over 14 days old, and tells you what it removed.
Runs entirely in your browser. No data leaves your device except direct calls to OpenStreetMap and, if you choose, your own Google account.
